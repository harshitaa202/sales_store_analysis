# sales_store_analysis
# store background
A Retail store chain tracks daily sales transactions, including order details, customer info, product categories, order times, and order status. The business wants to optimize operations, improve customer experience, and increase profitability using data-driven decisions.

# Problem statement 
The store doesn't have a clear idea about:-
1. which products sell the most,
2. customers prefernce,
3. which items bring in the most profit, and
4. where things are going wrong in delivery or operations.
Due to which they are missing chances to earn more, losingg customers, and making poor business decisions.

# Solutions
They need proper reports and simple insights to understand their sales, customers, product and perform better.
